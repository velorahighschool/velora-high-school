How to deal with school challenges

School can bring new challenges, and small practical steps can help students manage them.

Exam stress: Make a realistic study plan and break tasks into smaller steps.

Anxiety: Talk with a trusted adult, teacher, or school counsellor.

School adaptation: Join an activity and introduce yourself to classmates.

Difficult subjects: Ask your teacher questions and arrange extra practice.

Relaxation: Make time for sports, art, music, and rest.
